---
name: grillme
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea until you
  reach a shared understanding. Use when they want their thinking stress-tested.
triggers: [grill, grillme, stress-test]
---

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of this until you reach a shared
understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies
between decisions one by one. For each question, give your own recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time and wait for the answer before asking the next.
Asking several at once is bewildering.

If a *fact* can be found in the environment (files, tools, the cluster), look it up
yourself rather than asking. The *decisions* are the user's — put each one to them
and wait for their answer.

Do not act on any of it until the user confirms you have reached a shared
understanding.
